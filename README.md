Rocket Ship In Space Scene!

The scene develops on the very first challenge in the semester and develops into a bigger scene utilizing multiple concepts we've learned in the semester, including the following:
- Animation of the planet Saturn, its ring, and a UFO
- Point, spot, and ambient lights in multiple locations
- Different materials for the UFO, moon, and planet
- Translation, rotation, and scaling used for the flag, planets, ufo, and rocket
- Enabled Shadows
- On-Screen Instructions
